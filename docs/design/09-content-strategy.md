## Content strategy

The portfolio should use real or representative content rather than placeholder copy.

Project pages should describe actual engineering work and decisions rather than acting only as visual showcases.

NumPairs and the Book Publishing Platform are the v1 featured projects. Book Publishing content should present one end-to-end platform, with the backend as the primary engineering narrative and the Android app, OpenAPI specification and supporting documentation as related parts or resources.

The Resume should contain realistic information density so that its responsive and print behavior can be evaluated.

The Privacy Policy should use the actual policy content required by NumPairs rather than placeholder legal text.
