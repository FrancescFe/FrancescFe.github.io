## Footer

The footer is a consistent visual pattern across the portfolio.

Its content is deliberately limited to:

**Left**

- copyright information.

**Right**

- GitHub;
- LinkedIn.

Email is intentionally excluded.

The footer should remain visually secondary to page content while providing predictable access to external professional profiles.

## Scroll-to-top interaction

Pages containing significant vertical content provide a **scroll-to-top control**.

The control:

- remains hidden while the user is near the beginning of the page;
- appears after the user has scrolled sufficiently away from the top;
- is positioned in the lower-right area of the viewport;
- returns the user to the beginning of the page;
- remains visually accessible without obscuring important content.

The control must be keyboard accessible and expose an appropriate accessible name.

Scrolling behavior must respect the user's reduced-motion preference. Smooth scrolling must not be required when reduced motion is requested.

The exact visibility threshold, positioning values, and motion behavior will be formalized during Stage 2.
