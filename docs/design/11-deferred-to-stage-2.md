## Deferred to Stage 2

The mockups establish visual direction and interaction intent, but the following implementation-level decisions remain intentionally open:

- definitive semantic color tokens;
- complete typography scale;
- spacing scale;
- responsive breakpoints;
- maximum content widths;
- border and radius tokens;
- button variants;
- link states;
- project-card variants;
- project-detail content patterns;
- navigation component specification;
- focus-ring specification;
- hover, active, and disabled states;
- scroll-to-top visibility threshold and positioning details;
- anchor scrolling behavior;
- animation durations and easing;
- final responsive behavior of dense Resume content.

These decisions should be derived from the approved mockups rather than introducing a new visual direction.
